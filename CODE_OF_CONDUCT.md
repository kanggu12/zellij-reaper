# Code of conduct

zellij-reaper follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique the work and not the person, and leave room for people who are new
to open source or who write English as a second language.

To report behavior that breaks it, contact the maintainer privately: open the repository's **Security** tab, choose
**Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct". Only the maintainer sees it. Reports are handled in
confidence, and the steps follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
